# Omniverse Evidence

Omniverse is an approved MajorMUD Reforged research source.

## Evidence policy
- Preserve source artifacts unchanged.
- Omniverse material is not automatically canonical.
- Separate stock/Classic research from intentional OmniMUD enhancements.
- Verify claims against imported databases, DLL evidence, MBBSEmu, MM Explorer, simulators, or reproducible runtime behavior.
- Promote only verified findings into the Librarium with provenance.

## Structure
- `ClassicResearch/`: claims about stock MajorMUD behavior or data.
- `OmniFeatures/`: Omniverse/OmniMUD-specific features and intentional changes.
- `Discord/`, `Documentation/`, `Websites/`, `Videos/`, `MessageExports/`: raw source intake.
- `Analysis/`: Reforged interpretation that has not yet been promoted.
- `Verification/`: cross-source comparison and validation reports.
- `ValidationPasses/`: dedicated OVGN validation records.
