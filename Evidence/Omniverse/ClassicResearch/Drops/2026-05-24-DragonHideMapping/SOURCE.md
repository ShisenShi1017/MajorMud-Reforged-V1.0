- Source: Omniverse Discord
- Author: Omniverse owner/developer and community participants
- Source date: 2026-05-23 through 2026-05-24
- Category: Classic Research with a documented OmniMUD adjustment discussion

## Investigation represented
- A player reported white dragon hides dropping only from young dragons at 1%.
- The discussion distinguished preserving stock MajorMUD from making an OmniMUD adjustment.
- Legacy records were inspected for black, blue, green, red, and white dragons.
- The investigation found records where a drop percentage existed but no hide item was assigned.
- A later mapping shown in the screenshots assigns hide items to additional dragon age variants.

## Important distinction
The screenshots contain both observations of stock legacy data and proposed/applied OmniMUD corrections. Do not treat the adjusted mapping as stock Classic without independent verification.
