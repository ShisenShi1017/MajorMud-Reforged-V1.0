# Librarium Governing Principles

## 1. Librarium Is Canonical

The Librarium is the single source of truth for MajorMUD Reforged. Canonical content, formulas, relationships, evidence, world overlays, validation status, and runtime meaning belong in `/Librarium`.

## 2. `/Data` Is Generated Output

`/Data` exists for runtime compatibility and generated world packages. It is not the master copy. Treat `/Data` as compiled/exported output from Librarium knowledge.

## 3. Imported Evidence Is Retained Until Completion

`/ImportedDatabases` remains in the repository until the Librarium is 100% complete and the user explicitly approves retirement. It is retained for formula discovery, provenance, re-mining, and conflict resolution.

## 4. Host Edits; Client Reads

The host/server/editor may edit validated Librarium world definitions and overlays. The client receives read-only access to the active world context: Classic, Custom, or AI-driven.

## 5. Classic Is Locked

Classic definitions are evidence-backed and protected. Custom and AI worlds must use explicit overlays/overrides rather than corrupting Classic canon.

## 6. Every Fact Requires Evidence Metadata

Every entity, relationship, assertion, formula, behavior, and world override should carry evidence state, confidence, provenance/source, relationships, and validation status.

## 7. Runtime/Server/Editor Are Consumers

Runtime, server, editor, client, validation tools, and AI systems must not invent canonical game truth independently. They consume Librarium records, generated runtime output, or approved APIs.

## 8. Promotion Requires Evidence

Knowledge moves through the lifecycle: `Hypothesis → Observed → Derived → Verified`. Promotion requires recorded evidence. Do not silently promote assumptions.

## 9. Keep The Documents

Architecture, evidence, reports, handoffs, and decision records are part of the project memory and must be kept aligned with the Librarium-first model.
