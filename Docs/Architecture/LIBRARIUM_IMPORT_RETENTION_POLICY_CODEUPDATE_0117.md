# Librarium Import Retention Policy - CodeUpdate 0117

The Librarium is authoritative, but imported source repositories remain required until Librarium completion reaches 100%.

## Storage roles

- `/Librarium`: authoritative game knowledge and world branches.
- `/Data`: generated runtime output per world.
- `/ImportedDatabases`: retained evidence/source archive used for formula discovery.
- `/Evidence`: reports, indexes, source-priority notes, citations, and mining results.

## Why imports remain

Until every gameplay-relevant formula, field, mechanic, flag, and behavior has been represented in the Librarium with evidence and confidence, the original imported sources are still needed for verification.

## Removal rule

Do not delete `/ImportedDatabases` until a future explicit milestone declares the Librarium 100% complete and source removal is approved.
