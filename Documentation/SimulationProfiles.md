# Simulation Profiles and Evidence Policy

`/Data` remains the runtime content source of truth. DataSimVer executes expected-model simulations over `/Data` without promoting or changing data.

A successful diagnostic simulation does not prove original MajorMUD runtime behavior. It identifies behavior that should be compared later against reverse-engineered Worldgroup/MajorMUD or MBBSEmu findings.

Future work should attach confirmed engine details to EngineKnowledge.md with provenance.
