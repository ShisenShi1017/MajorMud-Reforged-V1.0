# Search, Hidden State, Ground Items, and Cleanup

- SEARCH rerolls the relevant perception/concealment check; an earlier failure does not permanently prove nothing is present.
- Hidden discovery is per player. Targeted search and class/stat-specific behavior require their own evidence.
- Ground items and currency are room contents; Classic does not create corpses for ordinary monster drops.
- Identical visible items stack under Classic rules. Hidden and visible stacks remain distinct until the specified cleanup or pickup/drop behavior reconnects them.
- Cleanup can hide ground contents and delete items specifically flagged for deletion; inventories, qualifying gang houses, and shops follow their own rules.
